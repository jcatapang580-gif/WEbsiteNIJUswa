GCAS 04

GCAS 04 also known as Readings in Philippine History this a subject that focuses on the study of Philippine history through various readings and sources. It explores significant events, figures, and themes in the history of the Philippines, providing students with a deeper understanding of the country's past and its impact on contemporary society. The course encourages critical analysis of historical narratives and promotes an appreciation for the rich cultural heritage of the Philippines.
