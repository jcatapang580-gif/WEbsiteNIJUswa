IT 211

IT 211 is a course that focuses on Human Interaction, particularly Human–Computer Interaction (HCI). It explores how people interact with technology and how systems can be designed to be more user-friendly, efficient, and accessible. The course covers topics such as user-centered design, usability principles, interface design, user experience (UX), and evaluation methods. Through discussions and practical activities, students learn how to design digital systems that meet user needs while improving satisfaction and overall interaction.
