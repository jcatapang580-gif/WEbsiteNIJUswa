GCAS 20

GCAS 20 also known as NSTP (National Service Training Program) this a subject that focuses on the development of civic consciousness and defense preparedness among the youth. It aims to enhance students' understanding of their roles and responsibilities as citizens, promoting patriotism, nationalism, and community service. The course includes activities such as physical fitness training, leadership development, and community immersion, fostering a sense of social responsibility and national pride among students.
