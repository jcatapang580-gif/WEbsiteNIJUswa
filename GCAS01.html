GCAS 01

GCAS 01 also known as art apreciation this a subject that focuses on the appreciation of art and its various forms, including visual arts, music, literature, theater, and dance. It explores the historical, cultural, and social contexts of art, as well as the techniques and styles used by artists. The course encourages students to develop critical thinking skills and an understanding of how art reflects and influences society.
